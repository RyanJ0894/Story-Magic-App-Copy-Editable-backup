COMPENSATION MODEL
Section Headline:
How You Get Paid
The Voice Actor Revenue Pool
Every month, 15% of Story Magic's total revenue goes into the Voice Actor Revenue Pool. This pool is distributed among all voice actors based on how often their voices were used.
Here's how it works:

Your voice is used in a project → You earn 1 usage credit
At month's end, we calculate your share of the pool based on your percentage of total usage
Payments are sent automatically via your preferred method (PayPal, direct deposit, or wire transfer)

Example:

Story Magic generates $50,000 in monthly revenue
Voice Actor Pool = 15% = $7,500
Your voice was used in 150 projects
Total voice usage that month = 3,000 projects
Your earnings = (150 ÷ 3,000) × $7,500 = $375

The more your voice is used, the more you earn. Quality voices that resonate with users will naturally earn more.
Minimum Payout: $25 (earnings under $25 roll over to the next month)
Payment Schedule: Monthly, within 15 days of month-end
Payment Methods: PayPal, Direct Deposit, Wire Transfer

APPLICATION PROCESS
Section Headline:
Ready to Apply?
Step 1: Download Recording Scripts
We provide professionally written scripts specifically designed for voice model training. Download them from the application page.
Step 2: Set Up Your Recording Space
Find a quiet room with minimal echo. Use the best microphone you have access to. Test your setup before recording the full 30 minutes.
Step 3: Record Your Audio
Follow the scripts naturally. Don't over-perform or use character voices—we want your authentic speaking voice. Save files as high-quality WAV or MP3.
Step 4: Submit Your Application
Upload your audio files through our secure portal. Include your contact information and payment preferences.
Step 5: Wait for Review
Our team will evaluate your submission within 7-10 business days. If accepted, we'll send you a licensing agreement and begin training your AI voice model.
Step 6: Go Live & Start Earning
Once your voice is added to the marketplace, you'll receive a confirmation email. From that moment on, you'll start earning whenever users select your voice.

FAQ SECTION
Section Headline:
Frequently Asked Questions
Q: Do I need professional voice acting experience?
A: No! While professional experience helps, we're open to talented newcomers. What matters most is audio quality and a compelling voice.
Q: What equipment do I need?
A: At minimum: a decent USB microphone (like a Blue Yeti or Audio-Technica AT2020) and a quiet recording space. Professional studio equipment is not required.
Q: How long does the review process take?
A: Typically 7-10 business days. High application volume may extend this timeframe.
Q: Can I submit multiple voice styles or accents?
A: Yes! If you can authentically perform different styles or accents, submit separate recordings for each. Each approved voice becomes a separate marketplace listing.
Q: Is this exclusive? Can I still do other voice work?
A: Non-exclusive. You retain full rights to your voice and can work with any other platform or client.
Q: How much can I realistically earn?
A: It varies based on how often your voice is selected. Popular voices used frequently can earn hundreds or even thousands per month as the platform grows.
Q: What if someone uses my AI voice for something inappropriate?
A: Our Terms of Service strictly prohibit illegal, harmful, or explicit content. Any violation results in immediate account termination.
Q: Can I update or improve my voice model later?
A: Yes! You can submit updated recordings anytime to improve your voice model quality.
Q: What happens if I want to remove my voice from the marketplace?
A: You can request removal at any time. We'll remove your voice from new projects within 30 days. You'll continue earning from projects already using your voice until completion.
Q: How do I track my earnings?
A: You'll have access to a dashboard showing real-time usage statistics and monthly earnings projections.
